Privacy and storage

Updated September 13, 2026

Your workspace

PlanIt stores names, departments, aisle descriptions, shifts, tasks, tour notes, routines, and completion history in this browser on this device. Local use requires no account. PlanIt has no analytics, advertising, or Walmart system connection.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you connect Google Drive, PlanIt sends your workspace directly to a private application-data folder in your selected Google account. PlanIt requests only access to its own app-data folder, not your other Drive files. It reads your account identifier and email to show which account is linked and avoid syncing to the wrong account. No PlanIt server receives these records, and workspace data is not sent to GitHub.

The Google sign-in library loads when you open the connection screen, after the site's Google integration is configured. Google handles sign-in and consent. PlanIt keeps the access token in memory for the current page session; it is not included in backups. Google may require you to reconnect after authorization expires. Cloud sync runs while the app is open and authorized.

Retention and recovery

Local data remains until you erase it or clear browser storage. Anyone who can use the same browser profile may be able to read it. Downloaded JSON backups contain private workspace records and should be kept somewhere private. Browser storage and backups are not encrypted by PlanIt.

Google Drive saves separate versions for recovery and conflict handling. These versions use your Google storage and remain until you delete PlanIt's app data through Google Drive settings. Disconnecting a device stops syncing but does not delete local or cloud records. You can revoke access through your Google Account's third-party connections. Export your current workspace before deleting cloud history.

When you choose a cloud version over conflicting local work, PlanIt keeps a separate local pre-sync recovery copy. Settings lets you export that copy. The normal previous-local-save backup is also retained when available.

Hosting and third parties

GitHub Pages hosts the public app and receives ordinary page-request information. Google receives authorization and sync requests when you choose its connection flow. Their services have their own privacy terms. The offline service worker caches public app files only; it does not cache tokens or private cloud requests.

iCloud files

You may import or export a backup through your device's file picker and choose a cloud folder where supported. PlanIt does not provide automatic iCloud sync.

Questions

Use the PlanIt project issue tracker for app questions. Do not attach private workspaces, associate names, or account credentials to a public issue. PlanIt is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Walmart.
